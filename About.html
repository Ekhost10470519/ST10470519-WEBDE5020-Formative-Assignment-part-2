ABOUT US

About

Luthando Orphans and Childcare was founded in 2022 to provide safety, homes and a second chance for abandoned and neglected children. We work with local food businesses, volunteers and community partners. Our objectives include providing food and homes to children around CapeTown, partnering with woolworths and hosting monthly outreach events through donations to let the children experience fun child activities.

Mission

The organization strives to care for kids without a home or parent/s who can provide for them. They take care of the kids with all the basic needs and more until they are ready to be put into the world by the age of 20.

Goals

  • Make sure all the children experience what its like to have a childhood.
  • Help children feel loved and supported to forget their tough past.
  • Rehome children into safe, loving families.
  • Educate communities about responsible child orphan rates.
